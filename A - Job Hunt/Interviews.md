Preparing for a [Google](https://www.linkedin.com/company/google/) Interview? Here are some tips.  
  
Communication is key:  
- Think out loud during technical questions  
- Clearly explain your thought process  
- Don't hesitate to ask clarifying questions  
  
The number 1 issue I see in interviews is engineers thinking its a weakness to talk through their process. It actually gives us great visibility into how you approach problem solving. If you're stuck, it's okay to say so. Interviewers may be able to ask you different follow-up questions.  
  
Practical approach:  
- Think through the Q, start with a workable solution, then optimize  
- Prioritize solving the problem over finding the perfect answer  
- Show your ability to break down complex issues  
  
Many engineers worry about their solutions being overly simple and prematurely optimize. It's better to show you can solve a problem in some way then build on it and improve. Don't let perfect be the enemy of good enough.  
  
Be authentic:  
- Emphasize your strengths and what excites you  
- It's okay to express dislikes - honesty matters  
- Reframe nervousness as your body preparing for peak performance  
  
While its true that technical evaluation is a key part of our interviews, we also want to get a sense of what you're passionate about. This can help us understand if Google and the target team is a good fit for you.  
  
Positive framing:  
- When discussing past experiences, focus on lessons learned  
- Avoid speaking negatively about former employers or colleagues  
- Demonstrate growth and resilience  
  
I will also say, "don't give up". I'm entering my 13th year at Google and I can still remember that it took a few attempts to get in. Learn from your experiences, practice DSA exercises and ultimately believe in yourself. You got this.
